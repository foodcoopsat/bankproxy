## 1. Purpose of the Service

IG Foodcoops Bankproxy is a technical payment initiation and transaction aggregation service designed exclusively for registered members of IG Foodcoops and integrated with the Foodsoft system.

The service enables:

- Payment initiation on behalf of authorized members  
- Aggregation and reconciliation of transaction data  
- Automated financial processing within the Foodsoft ecosystem  
- Synchronization of payment status and accounting information  

Bankproxy is not a public payment service and is restricted to authorized members within the IG Foodcoops framework.

## 2. Scope of Services

Bankproxy may:

- Initiate payments where properly authorized by the user  
- Aggregate transaction data for accounting and reconciliation purposes  
- Interface with external banking APIs using secure protocols

All actions are performed strictly based on user authorization and applicable legal requirements.
